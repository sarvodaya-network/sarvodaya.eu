# Distribution of Ideas in Video Form  

*Flosha*

* Create inspirational, motivational videos that speak to the people of today in languages that are easy to understand for them. Use uplifting instrumental music in ways that it also speaks to the youth.  
* In these videos, the vision as a whole or singular aspects of our philosophy should be explained. Particular questions should be raised and answered, like a visual F.A.Q. in short forms that people will be able to grasp easily and digest one bit, one video at a time.  
* The videos have to be uploaded to a free, self-hosted and decentralised platform such as a peertube instance. Every video should very clearly end with the link to a webpage and any kind of contact info and contain a sort of watermark/logo and perhaps the web address as an overlay at the bottom of the screen; not for reasons of copyright, but for reasons of guiding people to where they can find out more, to get familiarized with the constructive program and to make it easy to connect with others. 
* Every video should also end with not only an allowance, but a particular request and call to copy it and to upload it wherever one wants and across all (a)social media platforms as one wishes, in order to spread the message. 
* In order to prevent manipulation we may need to also embed a link to our original self-hosted platform so that people always know that whoever has re-uploaded the video is not the original source of the video and that in case that any manipulation occurs, the original videos can be found in the central link provided.
