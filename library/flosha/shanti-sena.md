# Shanti Sena / Peace Army 

## Regular Work of the Army 


## Work of the Army in peace-threatening Emergency

* "You need do nothing at all but simply and peacefully stand fast." (Vinoba) 



## Sanction of the Army 

> "[Shanti Sena] would displease no one; everyone would welcome it."

It is essential to make sure that the Peace Army is welcomed by everyone. The Army is doing nothing but working for peace, non-violently fighting for peace, un-interestingly serving everyone, for the Good of All, in the interest of all and against none. This has to be completely accepted by every soldier and every soldier has to be trained in doing so.  

But it also has to be communicated outside and we should give people, who don't know about the Shanti Sena and Sarvodaya and who may be sceptical and critical of it, no reason, or as little reason as possible, to find the Army being of any concern, of feeling any kind of fear in regard to the peace soldiers, in regard to what they are doing, how they are behaving or even as to how they are appearing, which is the first thing that they see.  
Therefore, the uniform, if there is any (a simple sign should suffice, such as a blue scarf or a blue west), should be simple and not let them appear militaristic. It is crucial to be Khadi. 


## Organisation

Local Soldiers, Generals and Councils.  


**Councils:**  
* World Peace Army Council 
* Continental Peace Army Council 
* Country Peace Army Council (e.g. *All India Shanti Sena Mandal*)
* Land/State Peace Army Council (e.g. California, Maharashtra)
* District Unit / District Committee. 


## Country Committee

Members:  
> "A group of fifteen to twenty persons  
> who have complete faith in the power of peace,  
> who apply this faith in their own lives  
> who practice daily self-examination and constant self-correction,  
> and who have a measure of purity of mind." (Vinoba)

Work:  
1. First Task: It does not issue any orders, but gives advise to those who seek it. Occasionally giving advise without being asked. In order to do so, it will make "inquiries into the truth of the situation and collect the facts", "in the spirit of a mother dealing with her erring son", "with motherly compassion" and then advises them what to do.  
2. Second Task: To send Shanti Sainiks "to any part of the country where rioting takes place, or any disturbance of the peace". 


## Land/State Committee

* The work of the states committee would be to "see that Sarvodaya literature reaches every home" (Vinoba), "would have a publication committee which would make sure that not one house was left out."  
* "Its second work will be to supply any Peace Soldier or local Servant at once with any information they may need."  


## District Committee

Vinoba: "No one will give any orders to the district committee from above". 


## The Peace Soldier

**Minimum aimed for number of Soldiers:**  
* Peace soldiers must be "in numbers proportionate to the number of the population". 
* "one worker for every five thousand people"

**Work of the Peace Soldier:**
* "scavenging, getting homes to accept a *Sarvodaya Patra*, serving the sick."

**Cooperation with Lok Sevaks:**
* Peace soldiers are supported by larger numbers of Servants.
