# Third Way

There is a dichotomy between anti-profit socialist (pro state) and pro-profit liberalist (anti-state) views. Most conflicts in regard to economy are all about this dichotomy. One side thinks more along the one view, others think more along the other and some try to find a middle ground between them, which is just a mixture of both and acknowledges and works on the basis of the same dichotomy. But there is a third way, the one that refuses both.  

Some people are against the overpower of profit and rely for it on the state.  
Other people are against the overpower of the state and rely for it on profit.  

One side, the liberals, see, that the state is violent; thus they work against it and try to make themselves dependent from it, while making others dependent on them. They think it's a big crime that the state forces them to give them a part of their profit. But they don't think it's a big crime that they themselves abuse others for their profit, depend on them and make them dependent on themselves.  
The other side, the more "socialist" side, sees, that the profit-driven economy is violent; thus they work against it and try to make themselves dependent from it, by making themselves dependent from the state. They think it's a big crime that the private sector abuses others for profit. But they don't think it's a big crime that the state is doing the same, to depend on it and make others dependent on it.   
Both of them misses to recognise what the other side sees.  
Neither the state nor profit are in the interest of you or all.  

In your interest is true freedom, which is self-reliance in cooperation with others.  

Who makes others dependent on them, reducing their self-reliance, is a violent abuser. That applies to the state as well as to any profit-driven corporation. Who lets others make oneself dependent on them, is (willingly or unwillingly) a supporter of that violence and abuse.  

There is a third way. The refusal to rely both on the state as well as the refusal to build any kind of profit-driven company which is just another form of violence making use of people and the refuse to serve and work for any such company.  

What remains are independent, free individuals in mutual cooperation and deliberate, free associations that work for their own needs and not for profit. It can be as simple as that. This is the third way in regard to economical affairs.
