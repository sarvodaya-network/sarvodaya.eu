# On Service 

Work + Love = Service.   
Service is the work of love. It is the only kind of work that is in accordance with love. Every work that is done out of love, in love, through love, without any expectation for reward, can be rightously called service. And whatever is not done out of love, in love, through love and not without any expectation for reward, that can not be called service in the sense in which we are using it here. It has to be pointed out, that when we use the word love, then we mean non-violent love. This kind of love, love without violence, love without desire, may as well be described as Benevolence (in German as *Barmherzigkeit*, in Sanskrit as *Maitrī*, or in Pali as *Mettā*), but when true love is meant to be like this and free from desire, then we can just call it love. And in the same way, when true work is meant to be service, because no work should be done without love, then we could as well just speak of *Work*. Any kind of work that isn't service, is "untrue", it is dishonest work. 

Now, who in this world is a burden on others? Is a poor begging man a burden on others? Is a wealthy, well-earned man a burden on ohters? The answer is very simple: Everyone, who uses anything that has to be made by others, is a burden on others. And the biggest burden on society is placed by those, who use up most. The one with the highest needs and the highest expenditure is the greatest burden on society and is the most dependent on others. Poor beggars on the streets do not use up much. Their needs are simple and little and their expenditure is very low. They are a burden on society like everyone else who uses things made by others, but their burden is so light that it can hardly be seen as a problem. Their dependence on others is very minor and neglectible. Those on the other hand that are considered to be "rich", those among them who use up very much, whose needs are many and whose expenditure is very high, they are obviously a much bigger burden on society, and they, while considering themselves as "independent" and "powerful", actually depend the most on others, very clearly so.
