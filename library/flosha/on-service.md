# On Service 

Work + Love = Service.   
Service is the work of love. It is the only kind of work that is in accordance with love. Every work that is done out of love, in love, through love, without any expectation for reward, can be rightously called service. And whatever is not done out of love, in love, through love and not without any expectation for reward, that can not be called service in the sense in which we are using it here. It has to be pointed out, that when we use the word love, then we mean non-violent love. This kind of love, love without violence, love without desire, may as well be described as Benevolence (in German as *Barmherzigkeit*, in Sanskrit as *Maitrī*, or in Pali as *Mettā*).
