## What are seedlings?

Seedlings are the first stage in the growing process of a plant, where the seed is the initial stage, and the fullgrown plant — ready to disperse its own seeds — is the final stage.  
In the seedling the seed can still be seen, it has just broken up and the plant has started to grow out.  
It is the stage of a plant in about the first week of its growth and before it develops any roots.  

A plant can only grow in the earth. It cannot grow separated from the earth, as it needs nutrients, it needs energy to grow from which it gets through its roots that in the earth. 

But the seed contains some energy in itself. The seed contains a power to initiate the growth independently from any outer nutrients. And all that is needed to initiate this growth is a little water.  

Thus, growing seedlings is the easiest thing imagineable. Seeds are collected in a container and watered. The water is regularly replaced in course of a few days. Everything else happens by itself. 

But why would we do it?  
Some seeds can be eaten directly and they contain many nutrients. Others can't or they contain some anti-nutrients which have some unbeneficial effects. Seedlings improve both: 
* Seedlings most often contain more nutrients, more easily available nutrients in higher density than both the seeds as well as the fully grown plant; because at the stage of the seedling all the power that the seed contains is activated and given into the growth of the little plant.
* At the same time, anti-nutrients are reduced.

Many seedlings can be eaten raw without a problem. Others may have to be warmed up. But in any way, both "producing" as well as preparing them for eating is extremely simple compared to any other food. All we need are seeds.
