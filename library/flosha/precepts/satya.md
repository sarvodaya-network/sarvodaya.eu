# Truth

To vow truth or truthfulness on one hand includes to seek truth, to find what is true, commitment to truth. This includes speaking the truth and not lying. It includes being reliable, by doing what you promised someone to do, including yourself. It also includes to evaluate every idea, thought or sentence in a honest, impersonal manner and not to judge it based on feelings for or against the writer or speaker of these words; you have to be ready to find truth everywhere. The meaning of something is not more or less true with more or less integrity or purity of the person uttering it. But while overseeing the fault in others and acknowledging the truth in them, you yourself should obviously seek to diminish any gap or conflict between what you consider to be true and what you do. 

Thus, on the other hand and this is most often missed, truthfulness includes following and practicing what you have recognised as true. This is also called "integrity". If you belief something or are convinced of something, but you aren't following through with it and do not practice according to the truth you realised, you are not living truthfully. You also won't be happy and will always feel dissatisfaction due to this conflict between your thoughts and actions.
