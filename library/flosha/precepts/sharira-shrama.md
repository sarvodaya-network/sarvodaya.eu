### Sharira Shrama

Sharira Shrama unites the idea to not abuse others for ones needs, not let others work for what you can do yourself.
But it does also include the idea of "toiling so that you do not have to toil" of the cynic philosophy. This principle has a much broader application and meaning than one may expect. It has meaning not only for our individual life, where we delay tasks and duties because we always seek immediate gratification, it has also wide social consequences. In fact, our entire civilisation is build upon violence and abuse rooted in ignorance and absence of this virtue. 

In order to prevent a few momentary "toils" and "uneases", society creates a whole system of unease, a system of necessary dukkha. Practicing the virtues mentioned before would make half of the civilisational apparatus unnecessary and collapse. It would stop the machine. The machine can only prevail because the soul is suppressed. And where the soul reigns, the machine can have no grip.
