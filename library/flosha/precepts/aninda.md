# Aninda

Aninda means no bad speech, not speaking bad of others. Aninda is basically Vinobas twelth vow that he added to the eleven vows of Gandhiji.  
Whereas aswada is pure intake, aninda is pure expression.  
Just as asvada is included in Brahmacharya, but is still mentioned and vowed on its own due to its perceived importance, so aninda is included in ahimsa, but Vinoba perceived it to be so important that it should be mentioned specifically.
