# Asteya

(4) **Asteya**, Non-Stealing, in its negative aspect means to not take what someone regards as his own. Or in other words, to only take what is given. This too can directly be derived from truthfulness. If I take something that someone else regards as his own, I do as if it is mine, which is deception. In its positive aspect, non-stealing includes grateful receiving what is given particularly to me. Unconditional giving and receiving is very evidently the most noble way of affairs when dealing with the exchange of labour and goods, compared to buying, hiring or similar affairs that the world considers as just. As regarding its relation to non-violence: If I take something that was not meant for me, I may take it away from somebody else who actually needs it and for whom it was meant, thereby harming others. But it is also necessary to realise, in Gandhis words: "If I take [or am given] anything that I do not need for my own immediate use and keep it, I steal it from somebody else." Thus, Asteya is strongly related with Aparigraha or Asangraha, Non-Possession.
