# On Science in the teachings of Vinoba and Lanza

Vinoba coined the formula: 
> Science + Spirituality = Sarvodaya

In his view, science is necessary to bring about and a very important part of a non-violent social order, but only if it is combined with spirituality, which in his understanding of spirituality means that science has to be guided and directed by eternal principles such as truth, non-violence and compassion.

Lanza on the other hand had a very negative view on science and technology. But what Lanza is understanding as science and what he condemns about science is exactly science devoid of these principles. He rightly so asked the questions: What is non-violent education? What is non-violent economy? But he did not seem to ask: What is non-violent science? What is non-violent technology? 

In my view Lanza wasn't wrong with anything he said about science and technology. He only missed one perspective and one thought: What science can do and how science could be if guided by non-violence.

And if his followers would have been able to clarify this question, they could have avoided much of the conflicts that arose after his death over disputes of technology. Because they would have been able to use specific kinds of technology, by adhering to very specific principles in regard to it, thereby making their life - under which they had to struggle so much and that made it so hard for them to find new long-term members - easier, while at the same time keeping their precepts and acting in accordance with their rule; the rule would have been developed and interpreted in a new light very slightly and carefully instead of throwing it all over and getting rid of it.
