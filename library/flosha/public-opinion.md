# On Public Opinion

Public Opinion is ...

It pressures ...

[Add Vinoba Quotes from Swaraj Shastra]

Being pressured by public opinion is only a bad thing if the public opinion is in discord with eternal principles. It is a good thing if the public opinion is in accordance with them, as then the public opinion is supporting everyone to live in accordance with them. 

Thus, in regard to public opinion it must be the foremost goal of those who want to work for truth, love and compassion in society, to form the public opinion accordingly. They have to steadily teach the gospel of love without any coercion in order for the public opnion to arrive at a point at which it is considered natural and obviously reasonable what all those consider as natural and obvious already who live virtuous and in accordance with the principles, while this will be considered as unnatural and obviously unreasonable which is not in accordance with the eternal principles. 

Right now the public opinion is in some regards in accord with these principles, while in other regards in discord with them. He who believes in the absolute value of the principles, has to constantly try to promote correct understanding of the principles and what they necessarily must lead to on a social level when following them consequentally.
