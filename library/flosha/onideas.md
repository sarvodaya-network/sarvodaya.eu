# On Ideas

On the Power of Ideas

What to write about:
* Men die, ideas don't.
* Summary of what Vinoba wrote in Third Power.
* About the importance of literature.
* About the power of the poet/writer.
* The pen vs. sword.
* The idea/thought as the starting point inspiring change, therefore the place of ideas and the distribution of ideas in the work, at the foundation of the constructive program.
* a scientific approach to ideas, being ready to develop them further. but development does not mean changing them essentially; because any idea that is valuable, that is actually of value and contains a truth, is based on the eternal principles, is rooted in the eternal principles, and so can never be about getting rid of them, but development always only means to purify them, to refine them further.
* in the same way as it is our duty to refine our own understanding of the ideas and how to deduce relevant ideas from the eternal principles by applying them onto life and different aspects of life, it is also our duty to refine more and more the ways in which we communicate these ideas and explain them. Because apart from the idea itself and the value or truth of the idea itself (it has value due to its truth), what will lead people to accept the ideas, when they are true, is how easily they can understand them and how much we can help them to apply them in their lifes.
* The whole "garden of ideas" idea by Vinoba and how we have to be open to get good ideas from everywhere and accept truth wherever it is to be found.
