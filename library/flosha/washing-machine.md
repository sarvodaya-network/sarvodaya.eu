# Thoughts on Washing Machines

There are such activities, such human duties, that men and women usually liked to do, that weren't considered a burden. 

Spinning and weavimg are of such a kind. It doesn't necessarily harm to make these two processes more effective and faster and to mechanise them non-violently (that means *if* done on the individual or local level, deliberately, independently, in non-profit driven ways and so on). But it isn't necessary to do so, when it is not perceived as a burden, but as an activity that brings joy.  

Washing clothing from what I can say, is not such an activity. It has always been burdensome and I feel confident to say that there weren't many enjoying this necessity – but that shouldn't be considered a given and shouldn't prevent others to experiment in this direction and see if there aren't ways of handwashing that are more effective and enjoyable and are not as difficult to do. 

There are, for example, differences due to climate. When there is no cold winter with frozen rivers and when clean rivers or rivulets exist, handwashing is a different matter already. Where it is freezing cold and the water frozen in the winter, washing becomes a different matter. When all openly flowing water is dirty and toxic thanks to the abuse of the industry, it becomes a different matter too. Some cloth has to be washed with hot water and others shouldn't, which also makes it a different matter.  

However that may be: There were machines that caused nothing but suffering, abusive machines that weren't invented for the wellbeing of the people but only for their exploitation, machines which destroyed human life instead of serving it and which humans therefore fought against and wanted to destroy. Such machines were the spinning and weaving machines. 

The household washing machine however falls not in this category. And that is the case exactly because it is a machine for the individual household, affordable by all, at least ideally. Not everyone could afford it but eventually that was the goal and now, at least in some countries, it is in almost every household. It would have been the same with spinning and weaving machines developed for the individual spinners and weavers to aid them instead of destroying them for personal gain. 

That it is in every household, not just as one machine per family, but often even one per individual and in individual apartments, is very unnecessary and wasteful in itself, which has to do with the isolation that is pervading all of our modern life that is said to be of such a high standard. In actuality not only moral and spirirual standards are on decline, but standards of communication, community, togetherness, mutual help and so on, are declining too tremendously compared to the times, when the so called standard of living was lower, which obviously are factors which are of much higher importance for any real evaluation of a standard of living, than to do so merely by means of income and material advancement.  
However that may be – the washing machine is one of the machines which actually improved the quality of life for many and therefore it has been widely appreciated.  

But it is, in the form commonly used now, relatively ressource hungry, it requires much electricity. It is one of the principles of nonviolent technology that one seeks first whether something can't be driven by human power instead, as that would make the high relatively demands of electricity, which has to be produced too, redundant.   

In case of the washing machine, maybe it would be possible to connect it to a bicycle, a rowing machine or a running machine. When then someone works on it for 30 minutes or an hour, he could at the same time have washed the cloth. 30min to an hour of such endurance work at a particular heart-rate is generally recommended by exercise physiologist as an everyday activity for health, to basically everyone. That means that it is generally accepted and it is a scientific advise, to do such training and many people follow that advise every day.

It could be a very easy, connecting, social practice that e.g. there is a washing room in every house with a few washing machines that are shared by a few households with connected exercise machines. Then just as the households create a washing plan to organise who washes his cloth when, so as to use the machines effectively, individuals can meet here and plan to exercise together just as they are doing now, exercising in a commercial studio for which they pay and without getting clean cloth out of it.  

There will be many people enjoying this process and of course there will also be some people who for reasons of health or age will not be able to do it anymore - but there will be less such people because that very practice will help preventing such health problems. Nonetheless there may be some who are unable to. Then there would always be people who would happily offer some of their time and literal energy to do it for them. This would be beneficial to connect people and at the same time it is not naive to assume such a readyness, because by serving others in such a way, one would at the same time do something for ones own health.  
As I said, people are doing the same already, with less social impact. So doing this kind of activity while knowing that they do something useful by washing cloth and even something with social impact that helps oneself or others, be it ones own family or a neighbour, is very motivating, so much so that it could motivate more people to exercise, which would then also fight the sedentary lifestyle and the obesity and diseases connected with it.  
At the same time an individual will have to wash cloth just about once a week, which leads to a minimum amount of exercise. But such cardio exercise can be done and is recommended to be done several times a week, if not everyday, and so there will always be enough people, which, for reasons of their own health, will follow this recommendation. This will suffice to wash the cloth for everyone. 

By this solution two important aspects of human life can be connected and tackled together. Washing cloth and cardio vascular health. It is time efficient, it is socially connecting, it is a form of labour as a sacrifice, it is promoting health, it is productive and sustainable, as it drives the washing machines with human power with no external electrical input required. What else could we wish for? 

And now, let us see: Had others the idea before to connect the washing machine with such an exercise machine?
