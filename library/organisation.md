# Non-Violent Organisations, Non-Organisations and a Scheme of Decentralisation 

* **Sarvodaya Samaj**: A non-sectarian, non-organised, anti-institutionist, free association of men & women coming together on the basis of ideas, only being bound by a shared discipline of thought. The Samaj exists only through independent individuals sharing the same absolute values of truth & non-violence; there are no official representatives nor official memberships. The Samaj is represented by ideas only and by everyone who accepts, shares and attempts to express these ideas in his way of life. Meetings are held in form of assemblies, which may be called every year with no form of obligation, in form of free meetings of friends or independent study circles.  
* **Sarva Seva Sangh**: Society for the Service of All. A strictly non-commercial, non-partisan, non-authoritarian, non-governmental, apolitical (in our understanding of the term) and decentralised organisation for the service of all through the constructive program, with strict rules of operation and a strict code of conduct based on spiritual values. It is doing two things: (1) Applying the constructive program through direct service, which is direct action without reward, either based on the self-sufficiency of the individual worker or a group of workers, or, when support is growing and people can commit to full-time service, on the support of people through gifts for the sustenance of the workers. And (2) the distribution of ideas, to make the non-violent solutions known, to explain the constructive program, to inspire people to work, in form of books, flyers, online publications and so forth. 
  * **Local Servants or Service Groups**; the workers doing the constructive work all over the world.
  * **Local Distribution Centers**; they can at the same time serve as meeting places of workers and of local study groups.

* **Non-Violent Scientists & Technologists**: Science, in order to be non-violent, cannot be wedded either to the state nor to coporations and must be kept completely free from governmental or corporate influence. It must serve neither the interests of power nor the interests of money and can thus not be allowed to be dependent neither on government funding nor private funding linked to commercial interests. Scientists should pledge to never work for the government nor for any profit-driven company, they should work on a non-commercial basis for the wellbeing of society only. It is only in this way, that science and technology can be turned from the asocial and destructive force they are today, as they are wedded to power and money, into a social and constructive force, wedded only to non-violence and ethical principles. Scientists working on such a basis, may do so self-sufficiently or by forming new organisations, associations or foundations that function in accordance with these social principles. Their work amounts to:
  * **Research what is socially beneficial**: Research everything, prioritising that which is most needed and most helpful to society; international councils may give guidance as to what should be researched on the international level, continental councils, regional councils or local councils may give guidance as to what should be researched on the respective levels.
  * **Explain & distribute scientific knowledge**: Making the results of their research known and understandeable to the general population with the utmost clarity and adherence to truth, making everything freely available to read for everyone. 
  * **Develop Free & Open Source Technology**: Groups of researchers and technologists should form study groups to work on very specific technological solutions, which are constantly improved and simplified, in order to be given to society as open source blueprints for production of small-scaled machines in form of a constantly updated Open Source Technology Database, online and in printed form. <!-- These blueprints can be used by local manufactures or, to the necessary degree, slightly bigger-scaled factories, in order to build these machines and bring them into society. ## International Federation for Nonviolent Economy

A federation of all the local, regional, over-regional, national and trans-national organisations that practice non-profit solidarity to purify our economic life. It includes such associations as...

* International Spinning Association (or International Federation of Solidary Spinning)
* International Weaving Associaton (or International Association of Solidary Weaving)
* International Federation of Solidarity Agriculture
* International Federation of Nonviolent Animal Keeping
* International Federation of Sustainable, Solidary Builders
