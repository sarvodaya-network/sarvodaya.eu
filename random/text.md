Spiritual liberation does not require mediators or religious institutions. It may require some initial guidance, be it by teachers or scriptures, it may require friends on our path, it may require mutual help. But we are not in existential need for a priest or a temple. We are in need of direct connection with the source, from inside out. 

In the very same way, social liberation does not require politics or political parties. It requires guidance by science, it requires local cooperation, spreading into an international network, it requires mutual help. But we are not in need of a king or of representatives in a parliament worshipping and serving the interests of money. We are in need of gram-swaraj, of direct self-rule and self-dependency on the local level, from the bottom up. 

Reason cannot harm truth. It can at most help to reveal it more. If there is any untruth in religion, reason will only help to purify religion; the essence of every religion will remain and just make the spiritual light of their prophets shine brighter.

Vinoba:
> Sarvodaya is the social order, which stands on the foundation of the way of life which satyagraha denotes. To achieve this, we will have to bring together and reconcile different ideas and ideologies by averting opposition between them. Reconciliation ends all arguments and disputes. This will ultimately result in the equanimity of the individual and the social mind. I have called this ‘samyayoga’.

> First is the ultimate aim, which I have called *‘samyayoga’*. Second is the philosophy, wherein I stand for *samanvaya* (bringing together and harmonizing different strands of thought). Third is the social and political goal, which is *‘sarvodaya’*. And the method to pursue this goal is *satyagraha*. 



>  We should realise this and serve all, love all and earn the love of all before we depart. One who has earned money in this world, but has lost love, has gained nothing. One who has earned knowledge, but not love, has gained nothing. One who has earned strength, but not love, has gained nothing. Love all and earn the love of all — this is the message of sarvodaya.
