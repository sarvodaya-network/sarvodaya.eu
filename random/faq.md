# Frequently Asked Questions


## Why do you list our community under "Laboratories of Nonviolence"?

The communities or laboratories that we list on this website have been chosen by us simply due to their explicit commitment to non-violence or because they seem to live in accordance with the principles of Sarvodaya or particular aspects of it at least partially (even if they may not know about Sarvodaya as such or may not be explicitly committed to our ideas). We list them in order to draw attention to these islands of non-violent research and experimentation, in order to promote mutual exchange, in order to learn from one another, in order to inspire others. If anyone should not be happy with appearing here, please inform us about it, then we will remove you from the list, but we would be happy if you understand our pure intentions for making your community a part of the list. 

## Why are some communities listed as "Ashrams" or "Arks", while others are listed under the "laboratories", even if they too belong to the community of the Ark or describe themselves as Ashrams? 

We only list those communities under Ashrams or l'Arches who are explicitly and consciously committed to the ideas of Sarvodaya and practice it in form of the vows that Gandhi and Vinoba (and in a similar form also Lanza del Vasto in the Ark) prescribed. They are the few centres that may (still) come closest to the ideals of Sarvodaya (or are on the way towards it) and explicitly attempt to do so.  
Regarding the Ark: The Ark in Gwenvez is, at the moment, as far as we are informed, the only community of the Ark in which the members (still or again) try to live under the vows and according to the rule set by Lanza del Vasto. In this way they are the only community at the moment that we know of which can be considered being in Shantidas lineage.  
Please inform us if you know more about the centres we listed, if you think that any centre belongs or does not belong into this list and if you know any other centres committed to Sarvodaya anywhere in the world.
