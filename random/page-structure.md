

# Project Structure

* **Sarvodaya Presentation**: A page without the big left index navigation, it should not be overwhelming and full of information, but simply structured, with images, short text passages and lots of room to breath, with just a few links etc. It is supposed to serve solely to inform shortly about what Sarvodaya, Samyayoga, Satyagraha, Samanvaya are, like a presentation. It is supposed to be rather modern, beautifully structured, like a mosaic of images, text and free space, that you can send to every person you know just to show him, what this is about, and that should be translated in all kinds of languages, as many as possible. It may follow a sort of brutalist design in that the images and texts are all framed by big borders maybe. 

* **Sarvodaya Network**: The Network should be like a landing page similar to a good, simply structured organisation- or news-site, with images like in the archive at the top and some up-to-date news or articles at the bottom. One of the first and most prominent images should be a graphical link to the Sarvodaya Presentation or "Introduction". One of the images should link to the "gallery" section, which is our photo archive, one should link to the "library" which is our book/article archive. Other images may be the most important books or book recommendations from the library that we can make or of the newest publications; e.g. it would be good to have one good, short book dealing with the sarvodaya vision for a non-violent society or with our view on spirituality, religion and science etc. or a link to a Sarvodaya Glossary of terms.
* News should be a part of this Network and after one year or so they should become part of the library/archive. So only very important news may be posted here. 
* Very important is that the network should contain a section (probably right at the top too) that lists different communities, organisations, ashrams etc., so that people can connect to them or they can connect between each other. 
* Also very important is that the network should link to some sort of an international communication platform. This needs to be decentralised, therefore matrix should be used. To this end we should set-up a matrix server and invite people to join there. The problem is that matrix is not as ordered and structured as Discord, which would be much better for discussion, with these different channels where some can be used e.g. to set up a constructive programme. We can have moderators there and stuff like that. 

* **Sarvodaya library**: The library should be structured just as the Gothic Archive with a big navigation on the left, either with links to different categories/topics of books or to links of different authors (maybe both). And the image links should be either of specific books (e.g.) a big list of graphical links of the most important books on Sarvodaya from diverse authors without any particular order. Or image links to the different authors. Here too we may have both; so that you can have a startpage either showing authors or showing books, so that e.g. you could change between these two "views" at a horizontal top-bar navigation. Or we may have both on the same page, so that it may begin with a few rows of books and then a second section of images with a big of gap to the first where its only about authors (the images here should just have all the same square size and three in a row, so that they can be ordered easily). 
* When you click on a book, you just go immediately to the pdf of that book.
* When you click on an author, you go to a subpage about this author, which contains a very short biography and a complete bibliography, a sort of recommended list of books to read first, probably a link to the gallery section of this author etc.

* **Sarvodaya gallery**: The gallery is like an image archive. It should be structured just as the library, but here we may not need the navigation on the left and can do it with images only (also with a caption like in the archive or probably with a bit of a different style). Here too we can have e.g. images of people, so that e.g. by clicking on the image of Vinoba you get to a gallery full of photographies etc. of Vinoba. Or a gallery about Lanza, about Gandhi, about the Community of the Ark, a gallery about Khadi, about Spinning Wheels/Charkhas. But not only that. It is also supposed to just show all kinds of inspirational things. For example, beautiful hand-made clothing, production processes in craft, beautiful products produced by hand, all kinds of stuff. Here it is often needed that the images come with a caption. So for example in the Gandhi gallery we may often have a description that tells something about what Gandhi was doing here or which year this was or who made the photo etc. So every image needs (a potential of) a caption/description in case that we know something about the image. This way the gallery should serve not only as an archive, as a preservation of these images, of these old photos etc., but also for general VISUAL inspiration for workers and Sarvodaya enthusiasts that overall can give an idea or an impression of how such a world could look like. It is supposed to be the visual equivalent of the library, where there are just written ideas. Here it is about showing how this could look in pratice, showing what Vinoba did, showing how products can look, which are produced in such a way, showing how the society could potentially work, how buildings could look, tools, decentralised machines, all of that.
