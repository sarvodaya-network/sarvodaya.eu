

The Network aims to promote the ideas and philosophy of Sarvodaya, to preserve and distribute books and articles by Sarvodaya Philosophers and researchers and other useful ressources (like writings from other non-violent anarchists on whose ideas Sarvodaya was based on or who had similar ideas simultaneously). Thus, the Network tries to inspire individuals to take up social work (and offer education of how to approach that) by radical boycott of anti-social practices and parallel construction of social practices in accordance with the ideas of Sarvodaya, in order to create a post-modern, decentralised, autonomous, socio-economic order based on the principles of love, trust and mutual service.
